# Brevo

Contacts and lists, transactional mail and SMS, campaigns and their statistics.

## The key

**<https://app.brevo.com/settings/keys/api>** → **Generate a new API key** (v3).

## Good to know

Sending a mail, an SMS or a campaign asks for your confirmation; a campaign goes to its whole audience at once. The sender must be validated in Brevo.
