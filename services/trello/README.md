# Trello

Boards, lists, cards, checklists and comments.

## The key and the token

Trello wants two values. **<https://trello.com/power-ups/admin>** → create a Power-Up (any name) → **API key**: paste it in the *API key* field. Next to it, **Token**: generate one and paste it as the secret.

## Good to know

The token acts as you on every board you can see. Deleting and closing a board ask for your confirmation.
